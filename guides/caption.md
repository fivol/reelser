# Post caption

The text the owner pastes under the reel when posting. Suggested on acceptance; the owner edits and posts it. Write it in the language of the reel; `<project>/PLAYBOOK.md`, the brief and the owner's words win.

## Shape

```
Line 1     ≤ 125 characters (the part shown before «…ещё»): the ask or the context.
           The reel already carries the hook, so this is not a second hook.
           No greeting, no hashtag, no emoji as the first character.
Body       2–4 short paragraphs with a blank line between them: the context and
           the words people would search for, as normal sentences.
Ask        one: follow, save, send to a friend, or comment a one-word keyword.
Hashtags   0–5 specific topic tags on the last line. Never #viral, #fyp, #explore.
```

## Rules

- No links: captions are not clickable. If a link matters, it is «ссылка в профиле».
- Search reads the caption: put the two or three phrases a viewer would type (product type, task, pain) into the body as plain words.
- Emoji as punctuation at most, one or two.
- Sound like a person: no em dashes, no stock phrases («в современном мире», «не просто X, а Y», «это меняет всё»), no rule-of-three lists, no «подписывайся, чтобы не пропустить». Specific beats abstract.
- Under ~600 characters is plenty.

## Sending

Send it as its own plain-text message (easy to copy), after the final file: one line saying it is a suggested caption, then the caption itself.
