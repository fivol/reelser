# Hooks

Defaults for the first seconds of a reel. The owner's own idea, their words and `<project>/PLAYBOOK.md` win over anything here: when the owner brings a hook, keep it and only sharpen it.

## What a hook is

- Two hooks fire at once and they are different lines: what is **seen** (the first frame and its on-screen text, read in about a second) and, in a talking reel, what is **said** (about two seconds). On screen is the shorter, blunter one: six words or fewer.
- No setup. The reel starts at the sentence you would normally reach at second six: no greeting, no name, no logo sting, no «в этом видео».
- Motion from frame 1. A static frame with no text wastes the hook.
- Specific beats general: a number, a name, a real situation. «3 часа» beats «много времени». Never invent a number, a client or a result; if one is needed and unknown, ask.
- If the hook would work for anyone in the niche, it is not this project's hook. Put in the thing only this product or person can say.
- A hook promises something; pay it off before the middle of the reel.
- A hook never narrates what the picture already shows. It names the viewer's pain or sets up a tension.

## Formulas

Pick the ones that genuinely fit the idea. When pitching, vary them across the batch and name the formula in the pitch's `Hook:` line. When a version's hook is weak, try a different formula, not a rewrite of the same one.

| Formula | Shape | Works for | How it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold open | Start mid-action, the result already happening | demos, screen recordings, anything with a visible result | the result arrives after 10 s: cut to it first |
| Relatable situation (POV) | A moment the viewer knows from their own life: a message, a lost file, a question from a friend | everyday products, memes | a situation nobody actually has |
| Before → after | The old way, a hard cut, the new way; one lever named | anything visual | three levers at once: name one change |
| Time collapse | «Раньше X часов, теперь Y минут» | process and speed | a ratio nobody believes |
| Verbatim question | A question exactly as people type or ask it | search, FAQs | tidying it up: keep their words |
| Objection | «"<what they really say>". Ладно, вот как это работает всё равно» | people who already said no | an invented objection |
| Nobody tells you | «Никто не говорит, что …» + an uncomfortable truth | new audiences | a truth everybody already says |
| Negative command | «Перестань X. Делай Y» | saves, debate | a habit nobody has |
| Wrong way, not your fault | «Ты делаешь X не так, и это не твоя вина» + the fix | teaching | skipping the «not your fault» half |
| Myth-bust | «Тебе внушили, что X. Вот почему это не так» | authority, explainers | no real reason given |
| Permission | «Тебе не нужно X» | relief, saves | removing a blocker and offering nothing instead |
| Callout | «<narrow group>, это про вас» | cutting to the audience that converts | a group so broad it calls out nobody |
| If this, then watch | «Если у тебя <specific situation>, вот решение за N секунд» | qualifying viewers | a situation that qualifies everyone |
| The steal / artifact | A ready thing to take: template, list, tree, checklist | saves and sends | the artifact is not on screen |
| Head to head | A vs B, and one clearly wins | comparison, debate | not picking a winner |
| Own number + twist | A personal number or timeframe with a turn («12 лет делал в стол») | trust, build in public | a number that is not real |
| Flop record | «Первые N попыток провалились. Вот что поменялось» | beginners, honesty | the change sounds like luck |
| Receipt | «Я делал X N дней. Вот реальные цифры» + the real screen | experiments | numbers without the screen |
| Numbered with a favourite | «N способов…, №k никто не использует» (N ≤ 5) | lists, retention | N above seven |
| Mid-sentence start | «…и тут она пишет: …» | stories | never filling in the gap |
| Contrarian flip | A well-known saying turned around | debate | flipping a saying nobody says |
| Statistic | «N% людей …», sourced | sends | a number you cannot source |
| Reveal | «Вот новый …» + the object | physical or visual things | no stake: an advert |

## Checking a hook

Before writing it into a pitch or a reel, ask: does it read in one glance on a phone? Is there one concrete thing in it? Would the target viewer recognise themselves in the first second? Is it this product's, not anyone's? If the answer to any is no, try another formula.
